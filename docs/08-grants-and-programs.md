# Grants, Programs, and Free Support

Researched 2026-08-19/20 against funders' own sites and legislation, not aggregators. Grant-aggregator sites (grantcompass, hellodarwin, granthub, fsidigital) served stale data repeatedly during this research, including listing a fund that closed in 2019 as currently live. Everything below is sourced.

---

## 🚨 Read this before registering the business

**The WorkBC Self-Employment Services program permanently disqualifies you the moment the business starts operating.** No advertising, no transactions, no revenue before approval. If you register, promote, or sell a single bar first, that door closes for good.

What's behind it: orientation and assessment, ~10 weeks of business-plan development, then **up to 38 more weeks of launch support**, with **income support continuing at your EI rate throughout** — roughly 48 weeks of runway.

**Eligibility is narrow.** You must be unemployed (or working ≤20 hrs/week) **and** meet one of: currently on EI; an EI claim ended within 5 years; earned over $2,000 in EI-insurable earnings in 5 of the last 10 tax years; or receiving BCEA/PWD/PPMB. Plus a 35 hrs/week commitment, and you still fund all startup costs yourself.

Kevin's full-time Telus job rules him out. **Whether she qualifies is the open question, and it's worth one phone call before anything else happens.**

**WorkBC Centre Kelowna · 778-738-0225 · #107-1835 Gordon Drive**

If she doesn't qualify, nothing changes and we proceed as planned. If she does, we should sequence the entire launch around it. Note delivery moved from Community Futures to WorkBC on Jan 1, 2025; Community Futures' own site still describes the old model and is stale.

## The honest headline on grants

**Canada's small-business funding architecture starts around 10 employees, $300,000 revenue, and $200,000 project size.** A two-person home soap business sits an entire tier below where the system begins. That's structural, not a judgment on the business.

Do not run a grant campaign. The exceptions worth your time are below, and the biggest one isn't a business grant at all.

Two standing facts: **grant money is taxable business income**, and **never pay for grant information**.

## The largest cash item: StrongerBC future skills grant

**$3,500 per person, and both partners can apply separately.**

[workbc.ca](https://www.workbc.ca/find-loans-and-grants/students-and-adult-learners/strongerbc-future-skills-grant) · [educationplannerbc.ca](https://www.educationplannerbc.ca/future-skills-grant)

- Covers eligible short-term training at BC public post-secondary institutions.
- **2026/27 applications opened June 29, 2026.** 24 institutions, 250+ programs.
- **Eligibility: 19+, BC resident, active BC Services Card Account. Regardless of income.** No EI test, no business test. It's a *personal* training grant, which is exactly why it works for a pre-revenue founder.
- The Cross-Sector Skills stream includes **bookkeeping** and business analysis, mostly online.
- **Apply early** — the 2025/26 pot ran out before the new cycle opened.

$7,000 across two partners is larger than the entire startup budget. A bookkeeping certificate alone pays for itself.

## Free and genuinely worth doing

### WeBC business advising — free, unlimited, head office in Kelowna
[we-bc.ca](https://we-bc.ca/programs-services/business-advising/) · Suite 201, 1726 Dolphin Ave · 1-800-643-7014 ext. 112 · clientservices@we-bc.ca

Formerly Women's Enterprise Centre (the old womensenterprise.ca is dead). **"With the exception of our Business Plan Review Service, our advisory services are absolutely free."** No session limit, no revenue gate, no membership. Fifteen-minute intake form, then an advisor calls.

Convenient coincidence: **Community Futures Central Okanagan is in the same building**, #210-1726 Dolphin Ave, one floor apart. Both intake meetings in one trip.

Also free: **SMART on-demand courses, free through all of 2026** (start with Market Research and Financial Fitness), the Business Foundations Series, and a currently-running **"Make the Most of Market Season"** series that is directly on point.

WeBC **does not give grants** — they say so in capitals. Their loans (up to $150k at Prime+2-5%, or $50k via WEOC at Prime+4%) are real and you'd qualify, but borrowing against sub-$5,000 revenue is a mistake, not an opportunity. Take the free advising, leave the loan.

### CRA Liaison Officer — free, confidential, and explicitly not an audit
[canada.ca/LOhelp](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/liaison-officer-initiative-loi.html) · [request form](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/liaison-officer-initiative-loi/liaison-officer-service-request-form.html)

A free personalized session with a CRA officer who explains your obligations, walks through **available deductions**, flags common errors, and recommends bookkeeping practices. In person, by phone, or over Teams.

CRA's own words: *"the information you choose to discuss with a liaison officer will not be shared with other areas of the CRA."* Nothing said feeds enforcement.

Only **~10% of small businesses know this exists**. Of those who used it, 97% said it improved their understanding. **Book it before the first tax year closes**, so the books are right from day one instead of reconstructed later. Submit the form; an officer calls back in ~10 business days.

### BC Business Advisory Services — free
**1-855-698-2157** · Business.Advisors@gov.bc.ca

⚠️ **Small Business BC went bankrupt on December 5, 2024** and no longer exists. Its old domain now serves unrelated content — **do not enter personal or payment information there.** This provincial service replaced it. Calls aren't answered live; leave a detailed voicemail.

### UBC Small Business Accelerator — free, and unusually well matched
[sba.ubc.ca](https://sba.ubc.ca/) — free to any BC entrepreneur, not just students. They have an [Industry Overview: Cosmetic and Personal Care Manufacturing](https://sba.ubc.ca/industry-overview-cosmetic-and-personal-care-manufacturing) (noting every BC soap manufacturer is a small or micro firm, with a real niche for handmade small-batch goods), a Home-Based Business Guide, and an **arts and handicrafts guide containing a soap-making business plan example**.

### Okanagan Regional Library card — free, and worth ~$480/yr on its own
[orl.bc.ca](https://www.orl.bc.ca/using-the-library/get-library-card)

**LinkedIn Learning is free with a library card** — business, marketing, e-commerce and creative courses plus certifications, normally about $40/month. Highest-value free item found in this entire research pass, and it doesn't compete with the StrongerBC grant, so take both. The card also gets you the Business Plans Handbook (real sample plans from manufacturing firms), branch makerspaces, and free meeting rooms.

Not available at ORL, so don't plan around them: IBISWorld, Business Source, Gale, Data Axle. The free UBC guides above fill that gap.

### Two more free things worth ten minutes each
- **[ISED Financial Performance Data](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/financial-performance-data/en)** — free, no account. Benchmark revenue and expense ratios against real firms **by industry code and revenue band**. The relevant codes are **NAICS 325611** (soap manufacturing) and **325620** (toilet preparations). This is also the tool the CRA Liaison Officer walks you through, and it's the best free input for the financial section of a business plan.
- **[Canada Post Solutions for Small Business](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/business/small-business.page)** — free membership, no fees, up to 32% off domestic shipping plus a free label tool. Directly useful once the website starts taking orders.

### Other free tools
- **[BizPaL](https://www.bcbizpal.ca)** — customized list of every permit you actually need
- **[Business Benefits Finder](https://innovation.canada.ca/innovation/s/?language=en_CA)** — ISED's matcher across 1,500+ programs, five minutes
- **[BC business plan and cashflow templates](https://gov.bc.ca/businessguidestemplates)**, plus [BDC's free templates](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/entrepreneur-toolkit/templates-business-guides)
- **Community Futures Central Okanagan** free workshops and webinars via [myCommunityFutures](https://www.mycommunityfuturesbc.ca/) · 250-868-2132

## Worth a shot (time-boxed, low effort)

### Amber Grant — $10,000, cutoff the 31st monthly
[ambergrantsforwomen.com](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/get-an-amber-grant/) · **$15 fee, waivable** via info@ambergrantsforwomen.com

Three $10,000 grants monthly plus three $50,000 year-end grants. Canadians eligible. Fits unusually well: a dedicated **Startup stream for idea-phase and pre-revenue businesses under $10,000**, the **August category is "Hair Care & Skincare,"** and one application enters all three streams. Two questions, no business plan, ~90 minutes. Requires the woman to own **50%+** — document the split in the partnership agreement. Odds are low; do it once, then stop thinking about grants.

### Zensurance Small Business Grant — closes September 9, 2026
[zensurance.com/small-business-grant](https://www.zensurance.com/small-business-grant)

$10,000 plus five $1,000 runners-up. Revenue **ceiling** of $1M rather than a floor; partnerships eligible; BC included. Needs the business registered, and finalists go to public vote, which rewards a local following. Best odds on this list.

## Insurance and guild (near-mandatory before selling publicly)

**[Handcrafted Bath & Body Guild of Canada](https://hbbg.ca)** — the Canadian body for exactly this craft.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Professional Business membership (the selling tier) | **$75/yr** |
| $2M CGL package + $50,000 contents + cyber | **$500/yr** |
| $5M package | $800/yr |

Underwritten by Intact, brokered by BrokerLink (hbbginsurance@brokerlink.ca). Covers property temporarily at a **farmers market or trade show**, and lets you add **Additional Insureds**, which is exactly what market managers demand. Revenue cap $175,000. Group policy renews June 1.

🚨 **Order of operations — the CNF gates everything.** Professional membership requires **emailing HBBG a copy of an acknowledged Cosmetic Notification Form**, and the insurance only covers CNF'd products. So the sequence is **file the CNF → join HBBG → buy insurance → sell at markets**, and the CNF itself is only due within 10 days of first sale. File it early rather than at the deadline, or the insurance won't be in place for the first market.

🚨 **Two conditions that void the policy:**
1. **"Only products that have had a Cosmetic Notification Form submitted to Health Canada can be covered."** No CNF means the $500 buys nothing at claim time.
2. **If membership lapses, insurance is treated as cancelled even if paid in full.**

Other conditions: gross revenue max $175,000, US sales capped at 25% of total, and over 50% of sales must be bath and body. Confirm the current rate before budgeting — the posted sheet is 2025-26, the group policy renews June 1, and the $2M product currently shows out of stock on their store.

**Get two comparison quotes.** [Zensurance has a dedicated soap-maker page](https://www.zensurance.com/soap-maker-insurance) quoting from about $510/yr for $1M (the $2M price isn't published, so run the application). And **Acera Insurance at Capri Centre in Kelowna** is the local broker the Kelowna market itself points body-and-bath vendors toward.

Membership also gets supplier discount codes, a public directory listing, and Health Canada advocacy. Their courses cost extra (~$35). The members' forum is nearly dormant — don't join for community.

## Do not chase

| Program | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Small Business BC** | **Bankrupt since Dec 2024.** Domain now serves unrelated content |
| **WES Ecosystem Fund** ($100M) | Funds **not-for-profits**, never individuals. You receive it as free WeBC training |
| **Women Entrepreneurship Fund** ("$100k non-repayable") | Defunct since 2019. Aggregators still list it as live |
| **CDAP** | Dead. New applications stopped Feb 2024 |
| **RRRF / COVID relief** | Defunct. Only legacy repayment deadlines remain |
| **PacifiCan** (every program) | **$200,000 minimum project**, 10+ employees, incorporation required |
| **CanExport SMEs** | Needs incorporation, 3+ employees, **$300,000 minimum revenue**. Auto-rejected otherwise |
| **Canada Council for the Arts** | Soap isn't "fine craft" (clay, fibre, glass, metal, wood), and commercial businesses aren't eligible applicants |
| **BDC** (all products) | Every product requires 12-24 months operating **and** revenue. Use their free templates only |
| **CSBFP** | Eligible on paper, but no bank will underwrite a loan this small |
| **BMO / Visa She's Next / Cartier / Tory Burch** | **$50,000-$75,000 revenue floors.** Cartier also bans non-incorporated entities. Two are US-only |
| **Futurpreneur Core Startup** | **$5,000 minimum loan**, and requires creating full-time employment. Use the free Business Plan Writer instead |
| **Vancity Women Entrepreneurs** | **Excludes Kelowna** — Lower Mainland, Victoria and Sea to Sky only |
| **Coralus / Elleiance / Accelerate Okanagan** | Dormant, $1,320/yr, and venture-tech-focused respectively |
| **Craft Council of BC** ($78 with Kelowna discount) | Their jury categories are clay, fibre, glass, metal, wood, mixed. Soap almost certainly can't be juried, and their shop is on Granville Island. Email gi_shop@craftcouncilbc.ca before paying |
| **BCAFM vendor membership** ($75) | Voluntary, not required — and **their vendor insurance explicitly excludes soap makers.** Their own page bars *"vendors selling soaps/makeup/creams/baby products and lotions/essential oils."* The Duuo option is also being discontinued Nov 30, 2026 and doesn't cover products liability anyway. Join for the supplier perks or not at all |
| **Buy BC logo** | Agriculture, food and beverage only. Soap isn't food |
| **Nutrition Coupon Program** | Food only. Soap vendors cannot accept the coupons |

## Diarize for later

- **After first sales** — free [WeBC Kelowna peer mentoring group](https://we-bc.ca/programs-services/mentoring/community-based-peer-mentoring-groups/) (a $2,500 value, but pre-startups are ineligible)
- **~August 2027** — [BC Employer Training Grant](https://www.workbc.ca/employers-industry/funding-programs/bc-employer-training-grant) becomes available. Self-employed people **are** eligible (you name yourself as both employer and participant), reimbursing 80% up to $10,000, but it requires 12 months fully operational
- **Year 2-3 at $50k+ revenue** — BMO Celebrating Women Grant, Kelowna Chamber (their Chambers Plan group health insurance covers one-person firms and is the one benefit that justifies dues), Accelerate Okanagan Market Diversification

## WorkSafeBC

With no employees, partners generally aren't "workers" and registration usually isn't required. **Personal Optional Protection** is available if you want coverage for yourselves, and registration becomes mandatory the moment you hire. Confirm at 1-888-922-2768. Free independent guidance from the Employers' Advisors Office, 1-800-925-2233.
