## Etsy Canada

- Fees for Canadian sellers: ~US$15 one-time setup, C$0.28/listing per 4 months, 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 payment processing (domestic), GST on Etsy's own fees. **All-in ~10-13% per sale.**
- Needs: Canadian bank account, government photo ID, credit card.
- Soap is saturated for generic bars, but **the scent-free niche is search-driven**: people with fragrance allergies and eczema literally type "unscented soap," "fragrance free soap," "soap for sensitive skin" into Etsy. Long-tail listings + all 13 tags + natural-light photos. Winning title shape: "Handmade Unscented Coconut Soap, Fragrance Free Bar for Sensitive Skin."
- Category intel: "gentle/skin-focused" positioning outsells "pretty/scented" (goat milk soap is the category best-seller).
- Claim discipline: "for sensitive skin" is fine; "treats eczema" is a drug claim — see [01-business-setup.md](01-business-setup.md) §6.

## Own website

Kevin is building a self-hosted static site (see [../site/](../site/)) — $0/month on Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages, with buy buttons wired to hosted payment links.

**Payment link provider: use Square.** Square gives 2.9% + $0.30 online, and the same account runs the **market card reader at 2.65% flat** with unified inventory and one settlement account. (Stripe Payment Links work identically at 2.9% + $0.30 if preferred; Square wins on the market-reader synergy.) Reader: $59, often free at signup.

Skip for now: Shopify Basic (C$51+/mo — revisit when online sales clear ~$2k/mo), Shopify Starter (no real storefront, ~5% processing), Squarespace (USD billing + transaction fee on lower tiers).

**Domain:** .ca is ~$11-25/yr (Porkbun, WHC.ca, CanSpace) and signals Canadian-made.

## Local Kelowna & Okanagan channels

- **Kelowna Farmers' and Crafters' Market (KFCM)** — the anchor. Season Apr-Oct, Wed + Sat, ~100 vendors, plus a winter market. **2026 applications are closed** — contact Frances (application@kelownafarmersandcraftersmarket.com, 778-215-4498) about mid-season Day Vendor jury openings, and apply for the **2027 season** via marketmaestro.ca. Products are juried; **cosmetic vendors must show Health Canada compliance and carry their own product liability insurance.**
- **Craft Culture Holiday Market, Prospera Place, Dec 4-6 2026** — 200+ vendors, the Okanagan's largest holiday market and the highest-revenue days of the year for soap. **Apply now at craftculture.ca.**
- **Downtown Kelowna Winter Street Market** — ~17,000 visitors, oversubscribed; apply the moment applications open.
- **Penticton Farmers' Market** — casual day pass $40, published fee schedule; good proxy for KFCM costs.
- **Lake Country Farmers' Market** — Fridays, small, low barrier. Good first market.
- **Vernon** rotating market (no vendor fees listed) + Festival of Crafts.

## Wholesale / consignment (month 4-6)

- **Nature's Fare Markets** — BC health-food chain HQ'd in Vernon, stores in Kelowna, West Kelowna, Penticton, Vernon; 26+ years of explicit local-artisan sourcing. A scent-free sensitive-skin bar is a textbook fit. Expect ~50% wholesale.
- Kelowna gift shops and refilleries: consignment typically 60/40 in the maker's favour; approach with a one-page line sheet.
- **Wine-country angle:** Kelowna-Naramata boutique hotels, B&Bs, and spas buy guest amenities, and "unscented, natural, BC-made" solves a real procurement problem (scent-sensitivity complaints). Mini/guest-size bars at wholesale, direct outreach.

## Recommended rollout

- **Phase 0 (weeks 1-4):** compliance + payments — register business, PST, insurance, labels, .ca domain + matching Instagram/Facebook handles, Square account + reader.
- **Phase 1 (fall/winter):** local markets — Craft Culture (Dec 4-6) + Winter Street Market applications now; KFCM Day Vendor inquiry; casual days at Lake Country/Penticton in spring; KFCM 2027 season application. Face-to-face is where unscented wins: shoppers feel that "no scent" is a feature.
- **Phase 2 (months 1-2, parallel):** Etsy shop — 10-15 long-tail listings capturing existing niche search demand.
- **Phase 3 (months 2-3):** self-hosted site live with Square payment links; FB/IG shop tabs as funnel; QR code on the market stall → mailing list. Repeat purchases at 2.9% instead of Etsy's 10.5%.
- **Phase 4 (months 4-6):** wholesale — Nature's Fare, gift shops, B&B/spa amenity line.
- **Phase 5 (6+ months, conditional):** Amazon Handmade; Shopify if site sales clear $2k/mo; TikTok Shop if it opens in Canada.

**Core logic:** markets fund the business and build the story; Etsy captures existing niche search demand cheaply; the site converts both into repeat customers at the lowest fees; wholesale scales it.
