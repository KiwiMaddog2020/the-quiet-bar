The Quiet Bar
Working name, your call before we buy the domain
Kevin + you · Kelowna, BC · August 2026

A small soap company that competes on what it leaves out.

Truly scent-free, natural, coconut-forward bars, made by hand and cured six weeks. Four ingredients: olive oil, coconut oil, water, lye. No fragrance, no dye, no claims we can't keep. First sale target: November, in time for the holiday markets.

Why this wins
  • People search for it. "Unscented soap" and "fragrance free" are typed into Etsy every day by people with allergies and eczema.
  • Markets notice it. Among 100 vendors, ours is the one stall a scent-sensitive shopper can buy from.
  • Stores need it. Health stores and wine-country B&Bs want unscented, BC-made bars for sensitive guests.
