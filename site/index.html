Questions

Asked, answered, quietly.

Is it really one hundred percent scent-free? +

Yes. No fragrance oil, no essential oil, no "natural fragrance," no masking scent. The bars carry the faint, clean smell of the oils themselves, and it does not linger on skin.

Lye sounds harsh. Is it? +

Lye is how every true soap has been made for about five thousand years. The reaction consumes it completely, we cure every batch six weeks, and we test before anything is sold. What reaches you is soap, glycerin, and extra oil.

Is it good for eczema or sensitive skin? +

Our bars contain no fragrance and no dye, which are two of the most common irritants, and many people with sensitive skin choose them for exactly that reason. We make soap, not medicine, so we will never make medical claims. If you have a skin condition, ask your doctor.

How long does a bar last? +

Three to five weeks of daily use, if you keep it on a dish that drains. Soap that sits in a puddle dissolves. The dish matters more than people think.

Do you ship? +
